<p align="center"><b>A Video Player by Android Community</b></p>

TG Player is a video and music player for Android phones and Android TV. It puts everything you watch in one place, with one player:

- **Your phone:** every video and song, with a Continue-watching home screen and resume where you left off.
- **Telegram:** stream any video, video file or song from your chats and channels, with search, favourites, watch later and offline downloads.
- **Your home network:** play straight from PCs and NAS boxes (SMB), FTP, FTPS and SFTP servers, with servers found automatically.
- **Jellyfin:** browse your server, with progress kept in step and Skip intro.
- **The web:** play MP4/MKV links, HLS and DASH streams; files download ahead so they never stall.
- **Library:** your movie and show folders become a Jellyfin-style library with posters, ratings, genres, *Next up* and *Fix match*.

## Highlights

- **Player:** gestures for seeking, brightness, volume and zoom; double-tap to skip 10 s; lock; aspect and rotation modes; picture-in-picture.
- **Sound:** a 10-band equalizer with presets, a volume boost up to +10 dB, and built-in decoders for Dolby Digital, DTS and TrueHD audio that phones often can't play.
- **Subtitles:** picked up automatically from files beside the video, or found online on OpenSubtitles. Timing shift and four sizes.
- **Extras:** cast to Chromecast and Google TV, a sleep timer, A-B repeat and speeds from 0.5× to 3×.
- **Privacy:** history, favourites and settings stay on your phone; no account needed.

## Requirements

- Android 8.0 or newer, on a phone or Android TV with an ARM processor.
- Internet for Telegram, Jellyfin, web links and posters. Files on the phone play offline.
